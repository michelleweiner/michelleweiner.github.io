01 / Project overview

The process behind Happy Camper

A design-thinking journey from understanding the problem to testing and iterating on a more focused experience.

ResearchEmpathizeDefineIdeateDesignTestIterate
Design goal

Create one intuitive experience that helps families discover, compare, organize, and manage summer camps with confidence.

02 / Secondary research

Understanding the bigger picture

I reviewed existing camp research to understand when families begin planning, what they consider when choosing programs, and where the process creates friction.

Parents plan early

Camp booking research indicates that many parents begin researching six or more months before summer.

Ease matters

Registration friction can influence which programs families choose.

Cost shapes choice

Families need to weigh camp costs alongside the quality and suitability of programs.

Logistics are part of fit

Transportation, extended care, food, schedules, session lengths, and budgets all matter.

Takeaway: Camp choice isn't only about activities. Timing, cost, logistics, trust, and managing information all shape family decisions.

05 / Define

Turning insights into a clear focus

Research showed that parents didn't need just another camp directory. They needed a way to organize the entire planning journey.

Problem statement

Parents need a centralized, trustworthy way to discover and organize summer camps because the current process requires juggling recommendations, websites, deadlines, forms, and family logistics across multiple tools.

How might we…

Simplify discovery

Help parents find options without being overwhelmed.

Organize the details

Keep forms, deadlines, and payments in one place.

Build confidence

Make trusted recommendations easier to access.

Reduce stress

Support planning as a connected, collaborative process.

09 / Usability testing

Learning what needed to change

Five moderated usability tests identified opportunities to improve navigation, information hierarchy, and discoverability.

Finding

Navigation labels

“Campers” didn't clearly signal profile management.

Change

Clearer destinations

Updated the label and structure to match expectations.

Finding

Community access

Conversations were difficult to find.

Change

Visible community

Brought Community into the primary navigation.

Finding

Information hierarchy

Users looked for urgent tasks and deadlines first.

Change

Action first

Reordered Home and removed repetitive calls to action.
